The Phone That Knew Tomorrow
One sunny afternoon, a ten-year-old boy named Liam was riding his bike home from school. As he pedaled down a quiet street, he noticed something shiny in the grass.
He stopped and picked it up.
It was a phone.
The phone looked old, but the screen was bright. Liam pressed the power button. Instead of showing the time, it displayed a strange message:
Tomorrow: You will find $20 under the big oak tree.
Liam laughed. "That's weird," he said.
The next day, he walked to the big oak tree near the park. To his surprise, a folded twenty-dollar bill was lying right under it.
Liam's eyes widened. "No way!"
That night, he checked the phone again. A new message appeared:
Tomorrow: Mrs. Carter will drop her grocery bag. Help her.
The next day, Liam saw his neighbor, Mrs. Carter, carrying groceries. Suddenly, one of her bags ripped open. Apples rolled everywhere.
Liam quickly helped pick them up.
"Thank you, Liam," Mrs. Carter said with a smile.
The phone had been right again.
For weeks, the phone kept predicting the future. It warned Liam about rainy days, forgotten homework, and even a baseball that was about to fly through a window.
But one evening, the phone showed a different message:
Tomorrow: Your best friend will be sad.
Liam didn't know why. The next day, he found his friend Noah sitting alone at recess. Noah's dog had run away.
Liam spent the afternoon helping Noah search the neighborhood. Together, they finally found the dog hiding behind a shed.
That night, Liam looked at the phone once more.
A final message appeared:
The future is brightest when you help others. Goodbye.
The screen went dark forever.
Liam never saw the phone work again, but he never forgot its lesson. Sometimes the best way to change the future is with kindness.
